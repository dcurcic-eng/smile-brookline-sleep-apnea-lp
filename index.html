Symptom check

Sound familiar?Your airway may be the cause.

Obstructive sleep apnea happens when soft tissue in the throat collapses during sleep and blocks airflow. Each pause breaks your sleep and drops your oxygen.

The solution: oral appliance therapy

Sleep apnea treatmentwithout the CPAP mask.

A custom appliance you wear while you sleep holds your airway open. For adults with mild to moderate obstructive sleep apnea, or anyone who cannot tolerate CPAP.

Two routes, both offered here

Vivos oral appliance therapy

Developmental. Guides the upper arch wider over months to make more room for the tongue and the nasal airway. Non-surgical, non-pharmaceutical, and aimed at a lasting structural change.

Why it matters

Untreated sleep apneaaffects more than sleep.

Every blocked breath fragments your sleep and drops blood oxygen. Over years, untreated sleep apnea can lead to potentially serious health risks.

High blood pressure

Repeated oxygen drops strain your blood vessels.

Heart disease and stroke

Linked to a higher risk of both.

Type 2 diabetes

Poor sleep disrupts how the body handles sugar.

Drowsy driving

Daytime sleepiness raises accident risk.

Memory and mood

Brain fog, low mood and depression.

Strained relationships

Loud snoring keeps a partner awake too.

Good news: a 2024 study in JACC found mandibular advancement comparable to CPAP for lowering blood pressure.
